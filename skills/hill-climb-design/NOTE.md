# Hill climb design eng

Notes from the notebook (2026-11-07). This is the idea before it becomes a skill.

## The idea

A design gets better by climbing. Three models review it against a reference,
score it brutally, and the design is revised until it reaches a pass score.

## Two things needed

1. **A reference.** Something we already agree is great, like Linear. Every
   review and every score is relative to it.
2. **A council of reviewers.** The council produces scores.
   - Scoring
   - Brutal scoring

## The council

- 3 models. Each one takes the reference.
- Each reviews and gives raw thoughts, the "why" behind every judgment.
- A brutal score system. It takes a lot of work to gain a single point.
- Scoring also uses the reference. A point is only earned by closing a gap
  to it.

## How to make the design better

- Make 5 different designs, each a completely different type, so the user can
  feel them side by side before climbing starts.
- Then climb the chosen one until it passes.

## Files

- `RESEARCH.md`: references worth climbing toward.
- `LOGIC.md`: the review flow and loop.
- `SCORE.md`: the brutal scoring system.
